## 修正

- **亮星周圍的暗環**：星點小、噪聲多的資料（例如小口徑望遠鏡、智慧望遠鏡）縮星後，亮星核心外面會出現一圈暗環，看起來像荷包蛋。
  原因是量星點大小時把星暈和暗星周圍的噪聲也算進去，量到核心的 1.7 倍，縮星就縮過頭。現在縮星的力道有上限：不會超過星點核心本身的寬度。
- 一般素材不受影響：17 組測試素材裡 16 組的處理結果完全一樣，1 組只有很小的差異。

## 已知限制

- 還沒疊圖、也還沒解拜耳的彩色相機單張原始檔（FITS 標頭有 `BAYERPAT`）會被當成單色影像處理，請先疊圖再開
- 其他同 0.2.0：Windows 處理速度約 Mac M 系列的一半、Windows 上滾輪縮放稍卡、多濾鏡合成只能在視窗裡做

**English**

APU Processing 0.2.1 fixes **dark rings around bright stars** after star reduction.

Downloads: `APUProcessing-0.2.1-win64.zip` (Windows 10/11, 64-bit), `-macos-arm64.zip` (Apple silicon), `-macos-x86_64.zip` (Intel Mac). Each includes `Quick-Start.txt`.

**Fixed**: on data with small, noisy stars (for example small-aperture or smart telescopes), bright stars could get a dark ring just outside the core after star reduction. Star size was measured including the halo and the noise around faint stars (1.7× the core), so stars were reduced too much. The reduction is now capped so it never exceeds the width of the star cores themselves. Other images are unaffected: 16 of the 17 test sets give identical results and one changes very slightly.

Known limitations: unstacked raw frames from color cameras (Bayer data, `BAYERPAT` in the FITS header) are treated as mono, so stack them first. Otherwise as in 0.2.0.
