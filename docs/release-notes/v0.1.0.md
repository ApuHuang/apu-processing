APU Astro 系列的後製軟體：APU Processing（後製）。開檔就自動做完去光梯度、校色、降噪、縮星與星雲細節、拉伸，再用曲線、明暗、對比、飽和度微調。取代 APU Astro v0.9.8（Swift，只有 Mac），Mac 與 Windows 同一套程式。

## 這一版的做法

以 17 組實拍素材與人工後製成品為基準重新設計，每個步驟都用評分工具對成品量過：

- **拉伸依噪聲決定深淺**：色調曲線擬合自 17 張人工後製成品；預設深度讓星雲亮度與看得到的噪聲都跟成品差不多（中位數 ×0.91／×1.03）
- **降噪**：壓掉細顆粒與彩色噪聲、留下約一半自然的顆粒，不壓大尺度結構——不會變成斑駁或色塊（色彩細節保留 0.95，v0.9.8 只有 0.56）
- **縮星**：只處理星點本身、背景不動，亮星周圍不會有黑斑或黑圈（亮星黑斑 0.016，v0.9.8 是 0.038）；很大的亮星與飽和星保持原樣
- **校色**：用星點的平均顏色白平衡，不靠星表
- **背景保留色彩**（預設 50%）：滿版 Hα 星雲的暗處保留暗紅、反射星雲保留藍色；0% 是中性的天空
- 成品微調（明暗、對比、飽和度、去綠、分色版曲線）即時預覽；原圖／上次結果／目前結果切換比較；裁切、旋轉、翻轉不改原始檔

## 已知限制

- 校色不靠星表：星場的平均星色偏紅或偏藍的目標，顏色會跟用星表校色的結果有差
- 程式分不出暗處是光害還是微弱的星雲：背景太紅或太藍時調「背景保留色彩」
- Mac Retina 螢幕上影像的顯示解析度是原生的一半（放大到 200% 以上沒有差別；存出來的檔案不受影響）
- 2000 萬像素約 10–15 秒、3000 萬像素約 30 秒（Mac M 系列）

**English**

APU Processing, the processing app of the APU Astro family.
Open a stacked linear image and it is processed right away — gradient removal, color calibration, denoise, star reduction and nebula detail, and a noise-aware stretch — then finish with curves, brightness, contrast and saturation. Replaces APU Astro v0.9.8 (Swift, Mac only); one code base for Mac and Windows.

Downloads: `APUProcessing-0.1.0-win64.zip` (Windows 10/11, 64-bit), `-macos-arm64.zip` (Apple silicon), `-macos-x86_64.zip` (Intel Mac). Each includes `Quick-Start.txt`. All three are built by GitHub Actions and smoke-tested on a synthetic star field; the Apple silicon build has been used hands-on, the Windows and Intel builds not yet.

Designed and measured against 17 real image sets and their hand-processed results: the stretch follows the remaining noise (nebula brightness and visible noise match the hand-processed results at the median), the denoise keeps about half of the natural grain and leaves large-scale structure alone (no blotches), star reduction touches only the stars (no dark rings), color comes from the average star color (no catalog), and a Background color control (default 50%) keeps the faint nebula color in the dark areas.

Known limitations: catalog-free color can differ from catalog-based calibration on fields with unusually red or blue star populations; the app cannot tell light pollution from faint nebulosity (adjust Background color); on Retina Macs the image is displayed at half native resolution (saved files are unaffected); about 10–15 s for 20 MP and 30 s for 30 MP on Apple silicon.
